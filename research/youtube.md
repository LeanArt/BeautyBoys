# YouTube research: WoW: Forever beginner leveling guide (Gnome Warlock MVP)

Research date: 2026-10-29 (launch Nov 4, 2026; beta Sept 17 – Oct 22, 2026).

## Method and limits (read first)

- YouTube watch pages returned a Google "sorry"/bot-check page from this environment, so **descriptions, chapter lists, comments and transcripts could not be read directly**. Transcript sites tried (youtubetranscript.com, youtube-transcript.io) returned empty shells. I did not try to get around the bot check.
- What I used instead:
  - Web search restricted to youtube.com. The search tool summarises titles, description snippets and some top comments. Points marked "(comment)" come from viewer comments and are unverified.
  - The **YouTube oEmbed API** (public, official) to confirm every title and channel listed below. Every video in this file was confirmed this way unless it is marked "unverified".
  - One third-party write-up of a Kargoz video (kami-labs.fr) and Warcraft Tavern's written warlock guide, for warlock detail.
- One data point was captured before the block: Kargoz's Affliction video was uploaded **Oct 3, 2026** with **148,304 views**, and the channel has **120K subscribers**.
- View counts and dates are otherwise **not available**. "~N days ago" figures come from search snippets and are approximate.
- Confidence tags:
  - **[F-beta]**: Forever content made during or after the beta.
  - **[F-announce]**: official or trailer content about the announcement.
  - **[Classic]**: covers Classic Era, not Forever.
- Volatility warning: creators flag beta-era changes more than once. These include dungeon XP nerfs (Oct 1) and buffs (Oct 8), the Oct 15 racial rework (Gnome Eureka was changed), and wand vendor prices being nerfed. Guide text should date-stamp any numbers.

## 1. Recurring topics (ranked by how often they appear across videos found)

Ranking is by the number of distinct videos found per topic, plus how often the topic shows up inside general "tips" or "mistakes" videos.

1. **Class / spec / race choice**: leveling tier lists (10+), race/racial tier lists (10+), "best warlock race" (3+). Hunter and Warlock are consistently top-tier for leveling. Gnome is the usual Alliance pick for Warlock.
2. **Bonus XP stacking**: Cozy Sleeping Bag (+3%, 10+ videos), Well Fed food (+5% XP), campfire buffs, Leatherworking Camp Tent, rested XP. This appears in nearly every "level faster" or "tips" video.
3. **Professions and early gold**: about 20 videos. These include profession tier lists, "Day 1 gold", skinning and disenchanting, a wand-vendor trick (now nerfed), and selling warlock summons.
4. **Beginner mistakes / tips lists**: 15+ videos, e.g. "10 beginner mistakes", "20 mistakes that will ruin your first character", "things I wish I knew". Named items include rushing quests, overpulling, untrained weapon skills, corpse runs, and wasting Legacy points.
5. **Dungeons while leveling**: about 10 videos on dungeon XP, plus about 10 Hall of Thanes guides. XP was moved from dungeon mobs into dungeon quests and has been nerfed and buffed during the beta. "Collect all quests before entering" comes up again and again. Hall of Thanes (Ironforge) is the first Alliance dungeon at about 15.
6. **Addons**: 10+ videos. Most named: Questie, Leatrix Plus, Auctionator, Bagnator, What's Training?, Forever Wisp (route addon), NodeSight, Gear Quest Forever, Dungeon Journal Forever, and RestedXP (free 1–30 routes; paid beyond).
7. **Camping system**: 10+ videos. A campfire has 3 profession object slots. Sitting by it for about 1 minute gives buffs that last about 1 hour. Camp objects can be upgraded with blueprints, and the craftable camp items are unbound, so they sell on the AH.
8. **Legacy system**: 10+ videos. Points are account-wide, but only **16 can be spent** at launch even though more can be earned (29 or 65 depending on the source). "Don't waste your first points" is the main message. The "Talented" perk unlocks talents 5 levels early.
9. **Launch prep / Day-1 checklist**: 10+ videos.
   - Rulesets replace realms, and the choice is **permanent with no transfers**.
   - Name reservation runs Oct 27 – Nov 3. Launch is 3 PM PT on Nov 4.
   - Set up UI and keybinds before launch, and plan the route.
10. **Merchant's Favor / Waylaid Crates / Azeroth Commerce Authority**: about 10 videos. Favor is a currency for buying recipes trainers don't teach. You earn it by filling Waylaid Crates. Reputation comes from Writs. Logistics & Co. (Horde) and Azeroth Commerce Authority (Alliance) are the same function.
11. **Rulesets**: about 10 videos (Normal, PvP, RP, RP-PvP; Hardcore comes later).
12. **Library books**: about 6 videos. Books give gear rewards, not XP: a necklace at 10 books ("Friend of the Library"), "Greater Friend" at 20, and a bow, crest or night-light choice at 25 books and level 30. Old Mage Library books give any class a blue ring at level 20.
13. **Dual spec at 40**: about 8 videos, mostly Shorts. It arrives together with the level-40 mount. Devs floated "maybe a cheeky thirty" in the official podcast.
14. **Weapon skills / trainers**: 2–3 videos. You don't start with all weapon skills, and each capital city's weapon master teaches different ones.

### 3a. Kargoz Demonology 1–60 (VI3h379QvSY): detail via kami-labs.fr write-up (published Oct 25, 2026)
- **Builds**:
  - Recommended for most players: **19/31/0** (instant Corruption, Grim Reach, Fel Concentration).
  - PvP 7/33/11, ultra-tank 7/32/11, dungeon 0/21/30 (Pyroclasm on Hellfire ticks).
  - Kargoz's own advice: delay the full Demonology rush until about level 34–35, and take Soul Link and instant Corruption first.
- **Talent order**:
  - 10 Improved Corruption 1
  - 11–15 Unholy Power 5
  - 16–17 Demonic Energies 2
  - 18–20 Improved Voidwalker 3
  - 21–22 Master Summoner 2
  - 23–25 Improved Sayaad 3 (the Succubus is called "Sayaad" in talent names)
  - 26–28 Demonic Brand 3
  - 29–31 Fel Domination / Demonic Sacrifice / Soul Link
  - 32–35 Demonic Knowledge 3 + Fel Vitality
  - 36–41 Master Demonologist 5 + **Demonic Pact**
  - 42–45 Improved Corruption to 5
  - 46–50 Grim Reach + Suppression
  - 51–54 Fel Concentration + Amplify Curse
  - 55–56 Improved Bane of Agony + Curse of Exhaustion
  - 57–59 Nightfall + Improved Bane of Agony 2
- **Rotation**:
  - Levels 1–4: Shadow Bolt, Immolate, Shadow Bolt. Get Corruption and Blood Pact by 4, after the Imp quest.
  - Multi-DoT: **Bane of Agony**, Corruption, a utility curse (Recklessness or Weakness), Searing Pain, Immolate, then wand to finish.
  - Keep Demonic Brand up on one or two targets. The pet tanks one, you take the other and heal with Drain Life.
- **Pets**:
  - Imp: levels 1–10. Weak damage, high mana use.
  - **Voidwalker**: Kargoz's leveling pick, because Demonic Brand fixes its threat.
  - Succubus/Sayaad: best single-target damage.
  - Felhunter: casters and PvP gankers.
- **Curses**: Recklessness rank 1 at 14 removes 175 armor, about +10% physical pet DPS at level 14. Weakness is strong early. Elements is weak early.
- **Wands**:
  - 1–20: Lesser/Greater Magic Wand, Smoldering, Gloom, Moonstone, Torchlight, Cinder, and more.
  - 20–40: **Gravestone Scepter** (29 DPS, used around levels 20–30), Consecrated Wand, Excavation Rod, and more.
- **Macros**:
  - Mouse wheel pet controls: up = attack, down = follow, Alt-up = attack focus, Shift-up = hold, Alt-down = stay.
  - `/petattack [target=focus]`
  - Felhunter dispel macro.
- **Tips**:
  - Life Tap for mana. Demonic Energies passes 100% of the tapped mana to the pet.
  - Resummon a low-health pet, since **soul shards are less scarce** in Forever.
  - Consume Shadows (Voidwalker self-heal) was buffed.
  - Demonic Sacrifice lasts 2 hours.
  - **Demonic Pact lets you summon another demon without cancelling it.**
- **Affliction vs Demonology**: Affliction is better against 3+ mobs (Siphon Life, stronger DoTs). Demonology is stronger single-target and can overtake Affliction on 2 targets. Affliction is still fine for "drain-holding" mobs.

### 3b. Cross-checked written warlock guide (Warcraft Tavern, has a YouTube channel @WarcraftTavern), supporting context
- Affliction has the smoothest early leveling. Demonology gets comfortable at Demonic Brand (25 points). Destruction is hardest solo.
- Affliction pull: Bane of Agony, then Corruption, Immolate, Siphon Life. Wand until 30, then Drain Life. Life Tap when health is above mana. At level 30, drain-tank with Improved Drains, Fel Concentration, Improved Life Tap and Soul Siphon.
- Demonology: send the demon in, then Searing Pain to brand. **Don't use Searing Pain before Demonic Brand, because it pulls aggro.**
- Wands: Lesser Magic Wand (level 5, 11.3 DPS), Greater Magic Wand (level 13, 17.5 DPS), Gravestone Scepter (29 DPS, from the BFD quest "Blackfathom Villainy").
- Bane of Agony is learned at 8. Demonic Pact is a level-40 Demonology passive. Incinerate is a level-40 Destruction talent ability. Drain Hope is not mentioned.
- Legacy "Talented": talents unlock up to 5 levels sooner. Matching video: "WoW Forever Beta: Unlock Legacy Talents 5 Levels Earlier" (tygarlily) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qv38dZi9klg [F-beta].

## 8. Synthesis: what the guide should contain (from YouTube patterns)

1. **Before you start**
   - The ruleset choice is permanent.
   - Reserve your name.
   - Launch is 3 PM PT, Nov 4.
   - Install a small addon set: Questie or Forever Wisp, Leatrix Plus, Auctionator, Bagnator, What's Training?.
2. **Why Gnome Warlock**
   - Warlock is a top-tier leveler alongside Hunter.
   - Gnome is the common Alliance pick. Explain the reworked Eureka and note it changed in the beta.
3. **Spec**
   - Affliction is the default for beginners.
   - Mention Demonology as the alternative: stronger single-target, pet tanks. It gets comfortable at Demonic Brand (25 points).
   - Dual spec at 40 lets you have both.
4. **Rotation by bracket**
   - 1–4: Shadow Bolt and Immolate.
   - 4–8: add Corruption and wand.
   - From 8: Bane of Agony pull, then Corruption, Immolate, wand.
   - Life Tap and Drain Life to cut downtime. Drain-tank later.
5. **Pets**
   - Imp for 1–10. Voidwalker from the level-10 quest as default.
   - Succubus/Sayaad for damage from 20. Felhunter against casters.
   - Pet keybinds/macros. Demons scale with gear in Forever.
6. **Wands**
   - Lesser Magic Wand ASAP (level 5), Greater Magic Wand at 13.
   - Gravestone Scepter from the BFD quest at about 20–30.
   - Train weapon skills (staves, daggers, swords).
7. **Soul shards**: easier in Forever. Use Drain Soul ticks. Healthstones, soulstone, summons for gold.
8. **Route**
   - Dun Morogh/New Tinkertown with the Grund and Gozwin free bag.
   - Loch Modan, then Westfall at 14–17, including the Sleeping Bag chain start.
   - Hall of Thanes at about 15: collect all 5 quests, including the hidden 5k XP one.
   - Deadmines next. Dungeon XP lives in the quests.
9. **XP stacking**: rested (log out in inns), Well Fed +5%, Cozy Sleeping Bag +3%, campfire buffs, LW tent.
10. **Legacy**: spend points carefully (16 cap). Leveling and "Talented" perks first.
11. **Gold and professions**
    - Gathering (skinning or mining) plus vendor/AH while leveling.
    - Avoid crafting money sinks early.
    - The wand vendor trick is nerfed.
    - Merchant's Favor and Waylaid Crates are an optional side system.
12. **Side systems**: library books (necklace at 10 books, ring via the Mage library at 20), camping, dual spec plus mount at 40.
13. **Mistakes to avoid**
    - Overpulling and rushing.
    - Not training spells or weapon skills.
    - Wasting Legacy points.
    - Skipping dungeon quests.
    - Unnecessary corpse runs.
    - Following stale beta guides (date-stamp everything).
